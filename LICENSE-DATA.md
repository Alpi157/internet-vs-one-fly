# Data license and attribution

MaleCNS v1.0 source data and derived graph, contact and soma-coordinate assets
remain CC-BY, as identified by the [official download page](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/).
The MIT license covers FlyBench code, not the underlying dataset.

MaleCNS is published by the Male CNS Connectome project, including FlyEM at
HHMI Janelia, Cambridge, MRC LMB and Google Research. Cite the
[MaleCNS study, Cell (2026)](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674%2826%2900942-6)
and [dataset release](https://male-cns.janelia.org/).

Source URLs and hashes are in profiles/source-lock.json. Browser asset attribution
and hashes are in apps/play/public/data/LICENSE-DATA.md and HASHES.json.
Original biological IDs and contact counts remain recoverable. The legacy soma
display uses normalized x/z positions with unverified anatomical orientation.

The anatomy assets are derived from official MaleCNS SWC skeletons and the
brain and VNC neuropil segmentations, also CC-BY-4.0. Exact input URLs, sizes,
retrieval dates and locally observed SHA-256 values are in
profiles/anatomy-lock-v1.json. See docs/anatomy-v2g.md for the data-derived
frontal frame, physical units, missingness, simplification and quantization.
The existing game display is unchanged; the anatomy assets are for a later view.
