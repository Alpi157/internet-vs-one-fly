# Candidate encoder card v0.1

The alpha encoder is an explicit **feature-level stimulation** candidate. It bypasses early visual computation and provides two population-level scalar ports:

- `LC4` receives a positive looming-expansion/angular-velocity feature.
- `LPLC2` receives a looming angular-size feature.

These responsibilities are model assumptions grounded only at the role level by the cited literature. The exact transparent numerical feature-to-drive mapping must be defined and frozen before M06 execution.

There are no raw pixels, learned encoder, hidden policy, or invented retinotopic body-ID assignments. No optic-column dataset is added in M04. The candidate makes no directional visual-reconstruction claim.

Threat bearing may exist on the sensory side for later stimulus construction, but it must never be passed to the motor decoder directly or indirectly.
